Our background

Zenji Pharmacy was founded by Nabbuye Cathy as a small retail pharmacy serving its neighbourhood in Kampala. As demand grew, the pharmacy expanded into prescription dispensing and home delivery, and today serves hundreds of customers every week.

Case study: the problem

Stock was recorded in paper books. Staff often could not tell which medicines were running low or close to expiry, leading to lost sales, expired stock being discarded, and slow monthly reporting. Customer enquiries were taken by phone and frequently lost.

Our solution

The Zenji Pharmacy Management System lets staff log in securely, record every medicine, search and update stock, remove obsolete items, and produce stock and expiry reports in seconds. Public enquiries from this website are stored directly in the system.
